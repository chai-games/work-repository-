About Me

Hi! I'm Chai, an aspiring game developer, game writer and character designer who enjoys creating engaging gameplay experiences using Unity and C#. I love designing gameplay and mechanics, building levels, and solving technical challenges. I also have a passion in writing stories, dialogues, and character backgrounds to create immersive narratives in games. As well as designing characters and visual assets to bring the game world to life.

My goal is to continue improving my game development skills and contribute to exciting projects in the gaming community as I explore and expand my knowledge in the gaming industry.
